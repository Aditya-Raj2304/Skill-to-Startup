Overview

Skill to Startup connects people learning to build a business ("mentees") with people who've already done it ("mentors"). This policy explains what information we collect when you use the site, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account details — your name, email address, password, and whether you're joining as a mentee or mentor.
  • Profile information — anything you add to your profile, such as your bio, skills, location, and areas you coach on or want help with.
  • Conversation content — messages you send through the platform's mentor-mentee chat, and metadata about video call sessions (such as time and duration).
  • Usage data — basic information about how you use the site, like pages visited and actions taken, so we can keep things running smoothly.

How we use it

We use your information to run the core service: matching mentees with mentors, showing profiles, enabling messaging and video calls, and keeping your account secure. We may also use aggregated, de-identified data to understand how people use Skill to Startup and improve it.

Messaging & video calls

Messages between a mentor and mentee are visible to both participants and are stored so your conversation history is available the next time you sign in. Video calls are peer-to-peer; we do not record or store the audio or video of your calls.

Sharing between mentors & mentees

Your profile — including your name, role, bio, and skills — is visible to the other party once you're matched, and mentor profiles are visible publicly so mentees can choose who to work with. We don't sell your personal information to third parties.

Cookies

We use a minimal set of cookies to keep you signed in and remember basic preferences. We don't use cookies for third-party advertising.

Data retention

We keep your account and conversation history for as long as your account is active. If you close your account, we'll delete or anonymize your personal information within a reasonable period, except where we're required to keep it for legal reasons.

Your rights

You can access, correct, or delete your profile information at any time from your account settings. You can also ask us to export or remove your data by contacting us.

Children's privacy

Skill to Startup is intended for adults building or mentoring a business. It isn't directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone under the age required by their local law to use the service.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make a material change, we'll let you know before it takes effect.

Contact us

Questions about this policy? Reach us at hello@skilltostartup.com.
