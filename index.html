The Skill to Startup point of view

You’re not behind. You’re at the interesting part.

Most startup advice starts with a blank canvas and a demand to think bigger. We start with the opposite: the lived experience, hard-won skill, or oddly specific thing you can do better than most.

  • ✓ Build around a real strength, not a trend.
  • ✓ Talk to people before you build for people.
  • ✓ Make the next step small enough to take today.
